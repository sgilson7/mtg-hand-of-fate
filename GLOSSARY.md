# Glossary

The source of truth for what each word in this game means. Strings, tests, the plan and the README use a term in the sense given here. The page shows a definition when a reader hovers the term. Magic: The Gathering's own words (mana, mana value, creature, instant, sorcery, enchantment, artifact, land, detain, overload, unleash, scavenge, populate, flying and the rest) keep their Magic meanings and are not redefined here.

Write each entry as a `### Term` heading, then one or two plain sentences, then an optional `Not:` line listing words that must not be used for it. A number a rule decides is a `{placeholder}` the core fills. Keep entries in alphabetical order.

### Bank

The mana a side has saved in a fight. Mana you do not spend stays in the bank for your next turn.

Not: mana pool

### Charisma

How well you talk your way through Ravnica. It comes from your armour, lowers prices, and opens some story choices.

### Deck

The cards you fight with, chosen from your collection. Lands are never in it: they live on the map.

### District

A tile that belongs to one guild. Its pool draws that guild's cards, and some quests send you to one.

### Fate draw

Hand of Fate's gamble: the cards are shown face up, turned over and shuffled, and you pick one.

Not: coin flip

### Guardian

The creature who rules a region from its lair. The first region's guardian is Niv-Mizzet, Dracogenius, in Nivix.

Not: boss

### Health

How much harm you can take. Your most is the sum of your armour and a base, and your health is your life in a fight.

Not: hit points

### Pool

The cards a tile can turn up. When you step onto a tile not yet drawn, one card from its pool is drawn.

### Reach

How far a land may be from a fight and still give you mana in it, counted in steps along the grid.

Not: range

### Region

The part of the map one guardian rules: three by three chunks of five by five tiles.

### Standing

How a guild sees you, raised by its quests and its armour. Some armour is sold only to those with standing.

### Style

Your standing with every guild together, from your quests and your armour.

### Supplies

What you carry to eat on the road. Each step costs one; without them, each step costs health instead.

Not: food
