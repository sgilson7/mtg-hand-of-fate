# Glossary

The source of truth for what each word in this game means. Strings, tests, the plan and the README use a term in the sense given here. The page shows a definition when a reader hovers the term. Magic: The Gathering's own words (mana, mana value, creature, instant, sorcery, enchantment, artifact, land, detain, overload, unleash, scavenge, populate, flying and the rest) keep their Magic meanings and are not redefined here.

Write each entry as a `### Term` heading, then one or two plain sentences, then an optional `Not:` line listing words that must not be used for it. A number a rule decides is a `{placeholder}` the core fills. Keep entries in alphabetical order.

### Charisma

How well you talk your way through Ravnica. It comes from your armour, lowers prices, and opens some story choices.

### Contract

A job a patron sets on the map: beat a named creature on a named tile within {steps} steps. Vraska pays gold and Golgari standing; a lapsed contract costs health and loyalty.

### Deck

The cards you fight with, chosen from your collection. Lands are never in it: they live on the map and make your land pile.

### Device

An artifact set up on a tile. It pays gold each time you step onto that tile, until its uses run out.

### District

A tile that belongs to one guild. Its pool draws that guild's cards, and some quests send you to one.

### Fate draw

Hand of Fate's gamble: the cards are shown face up, turned over and shuffled, and you pick one.

Not: coin flip

### Guardian

The creature who rules a region from its lair. The first region's guardian is Niv-Mizzet, Dracogenius, in Nivix.

Not: boss

### Health

How much harm you can take. Your most is the sum of your armour and a base, and your health is your life in a fight.

Not: hit points

### Held instant

An instant kept for later instead of read. You hold one at a time and cast it to answer a bad fate draw, slip past a creature, or detain Rakdos.

### Hex

A curse an enchantment leaves on a tile. It costs health each time you step onto that tile, until its uses run out, and it leaves you at least one health.

### Land pile

The lands a side may play in a fight, face up beside its deck: one a turn, any of them. Yours are the lands within Reach, made up to {pile} with basics of your deck's colours.

Not: land deck, mana deck

### Minion

A creature Rakdos leaves holding a tile he razed in the Riot. It fights harder than a creature met on the road, and the tile is freed when it is beaten.

### Mission

What a game is played for, chosen before the guild: the Dracogenius, which ends with Niv-Mizzet, or the Riot, which ends with Rakdos beaten or New Prahv fallen.

### Patron

A planeswalker met on the map. Its loyalty abilities are offers of help that move its loyalty, which it keeps between meetings; you can also challenge it to a fight.

### Pool

The cards a tile can turn up. When you step onto a tile not yet drawn, one card from its pool is drawn.

### Reach

How far a land may be from a fight and still be in your land pile for it, counted in steps along the grid.

Not: range

### Region

The part of the map one guardian rules: three by three chunks of five by five tiles.

### Shrine

A blessing an enchantment leaves on a tile. It heals you each time you step onto that tile, until its uses run out.

### Standing

How a guild sees you, raised by its quests and its armour. Some armour is sold only to those with standing.

### Starting deck

The deck a game begins with, chosen from the six its guild offers: the guild's own first deck, or one after a deck Wizards of the Coast sold for that guild.

### Style

Your standing with every guild together, from your quests and your armour.

### Supplies

What you carry to eat on the road. Each step costs one; without them, each step costs health instead.

Not: food
